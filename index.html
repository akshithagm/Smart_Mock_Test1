General Examination Instructions

Standard Proctored Examination Protocol

1 General Guidelines

  • The mock test clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer at the top center of the screen will display the remaining time available for you to complete the examination.
  • When the timer reaches 00:00, the examination will automatically conclude and submit your recorded answers.
  • Total number of multiple-choice questions: 10. All questions carry 1 mark each. There is no negative marking.
  • You can move back and forth between questions anytime using Next, Previous, or directly clicking on question numbers in the Question Palette.

2 Understanding the Question Palette Status

The Question Palette on the right panel uses standard color codes to indicate status:

1
Not Visited You have not yet viewed this question.
2
Not Answered You have viewed the question but not selected any option.
3
Answered You have selected an answer for this question.
4
Marked for Review You have not answered the question, but marked it for review later.
5
Answered & Marked You answered the question and marked it for review (will be evaluated).

3 Answering and Navigating Questions

  • To select your answer, click on the option card or radio button corresponding to your choice.
  • To deselect your chosen answer, click on the Clear Response button.
  • To change your selected answer, simply click on another option before submitting.
  • Click Save & Next to save your answer and move to the next sequential question.
  • Click Mark for Review & Next if you want to flag the question for re-checking later.
